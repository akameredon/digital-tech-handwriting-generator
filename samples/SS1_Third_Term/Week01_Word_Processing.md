**Class:** SS1  
**Term:** Third Term  
**Week:** 1  
**Topic:** Word Processing Applications

---

**Behavioral Learning Objectives**  
By the end of this lesson, students should be able to:  
1. Define word processing.  
2. Identify common features of Microsoft Word or Google Docs.  
3. Create, save and format a simple document.  
4. Apply bold, italics, underline, font size and alignment.

---

**Key Concepts / Definitions**  
- **Word Processor**: Software used to create, edit, format and print text documents.  
- **Examples**: Microsoft Word, Google Docs, LibreOffice Writer.  
- **Formatting**: Changing the appearance of text (bold, size, colour, alignment).  
- **Alignment**: How text is positioned (left, centre, right, justify).

---

**Step-by-Step Conceptual Explanation**  

1. Word processors replaced typewriters and made editing much easier.  
2. Students can type notes, letters, assignments and reports.  
3. Basic formatting makes documents clearer and more professional.  
4. Saving work regularly prevents loss of data.  
5. These skills are essential for school and future office work.

---

**Practical / Worked Example**  
Type a short paragraph about Digital Technologies, then:  
- Make the title bold and size 16  
- Centre the title  
- Change the body text to size 12  
- Save the file as `MyFirstDocument.docx`

---

**Class Activity / Guided Practice**  
Students type their full name, class and three sentences about themselves, then apply different formatting styles.

---

**End-of-Lesson Quick Test**  
1. What is a word processor?  
2. Name two examples of word processing software.  
3. What does “bold” do to text?  
4. Name two types of alignment.  
5. True or False: You cannot change the font size in a word processor.

---

**Assignment**  
Type a one-page formal letter to your principal requesting permission to form a Digital Technologies club. Use proper formatting.
