**Class:** SS1  
**Term:** Third Term  
**Week:** 4  
**Topic:** Presentation Software

---

**Behavioral Learning Objectives**  
By the end of this lesson, students should be able to:  
1. Define presentation software.  
2. Identify the main features of PowerPoint or Google slides.  
3. Create a simple presentation with title and content slides.  
4. Apply basic design principles (consistent fonts, limited colours, clear layout).

---

**Key Concepts / Definitions**  
- **Presentation Software**: An application used to create visual slides for communicating ideas to an audience (e.g. Microsoft PowerPoint, Google Slides).  
- **Slide**: A single page in a presentation.  
- **Transition**: The visual effect when moving from one slide to another.  
- **Animation**: Movement of objects or text on a slide.  
- **Design template**: A pre-designed layout that helps keep slides consistent.

---

**Step-by-Step Conceptual Explanation**  

1. Presentation software helps speakers organise and display information clearly.  
2. Good presentations use few words, clear headings, and relevant images.  
3. Students should avoid overcrowding slides and reading every word from the screen.  
4. Consistent fonts, colours and alignment make a presentation look professional.  
5. These skills are useful for school projects, seminars and future work presentations.

---

**Practical / Worked Example**  
Create a 5-slide presentation on “Importance of Digital Technologies”:  
1. Title slide  
2. Definition  
3. Benefits  
4. Challenges  
5. Conclusion

---

**Class Activity / Guided Practice**  
Students work in pairs to create a short 4-slide presentation on any topic from the Digital Technologies scheme.

---

**End-of-Lesson Quick Test**  
1. What is presentation software?  
2. Name two examples of presentation software.  
3. What is a slide?  
4. Give one rule of good slide design.  
5. True or False: A good presentation should contain as much text as possible on each slide.

---

**Assignment**  
Prepare a 6-slide presentation on any topic you have learnt in Digital Technologies this term. Use clear headings and at least one image or diagram.
