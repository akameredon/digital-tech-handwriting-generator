**Class:** SS2  
**Term:** First Term  
**Week:** 3  
**Topic:** Advanced Spreadsheet I – Pivot Tables and Advanced Formulas

---

**Behavioral Learning Objectives**  
By the end of this lesson, students should be able to:  
1. Explain the purpose of pivot tables.  
2. Create a simple pivot table from a data set.  
3. Use common advanced formulas (SUMIF, COUNTIF, AVERAGEIF).  
4. Analyse student performance data using spreadsheet tools.

---

**Key Concepts / Definitions**  
- **Pivot Table**: A powerful tool that summarises large amounts of data quickly.  
- **SUMIF**: Adds values that meet a specific condition.  
- **COUNTIF**: Counts the number of cells that meet a condition.  
- **AVERAGEIF**: Calculates the average of cells that meet a condition.  
- **Data analysis**: Examining data to discover useful information and patterns.

---

**Step-by-Step Conceptual Explanation**  

1. When you have many rows of student scores or sales data, looking at the raw list is difficult.  
2. Pivot tables allow you to group and summarise the data by subject, class, or any other category.  
3. Functions like SUMIF and COUNTIF help answer specific questions such as “How many students scored above 70?” or “What is the total score of all female students?”.  
4. These tools are widely used in schools, businesses and government offices in Nigeria.  
5. Mastering them gives students a strong practical advantage.

---

**Practical / Worked Example**  
Given a list of student names, gender and scores:  
- Use COUNTIF to count how many students scored 50 or above.  
- Use AVERAGEIF to find the average score of male or female students.  
- Create a pivot table to show average score by gender.

---

**Class Activity / Guided Practice**  
Students work with a sample mark sheet, create one pivot table and write three SUMIF/COUNTIF formulas.

---

**End-of-Lesson Quick Test**  
1. What is a pivot table used for?  
2. What does the SUMIF function do?  
3. What does COUNTIF do?  
4. Give one real-life use of advanced spreadsheet functions in a school.  
5. True or False: Pivot tables can only be used with numbers.

---

**Assignment**  
Collect the scores of 10 classmates in any subject. Enter the data in a spreadsheet and create:
