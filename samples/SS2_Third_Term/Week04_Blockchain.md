**Class:** SS2  
**Term:** Third Term  
**Week:** 4  
**Topic:** Blockchain Technology

---

**Behavioral Learning Objectives**  
By the end of this lesson, students should be able to:  
1. Define blockchain in simple terms.  
2. Explain how blockchain is different from a traditional database.  
3. State the main features of blockchain (decentralisation, immutability, transparency).  
4. Give examples of blockchain uses beyond cryptocurrency.

---

**Key Concepts / Definitions**  
- **Blockchain**: A distributed digital ledger that records transactions across many computers so that the record cannot be altered easily.  
- **Block**: A group of transactions that are linked together.  
- **Decentralised**: No single person or organisation controls the entire system.  
- **Immutable**: Once data is recorded, it is very difficult to change.  
- **Cryptocurrency**: Digital money that uses blockchain technology (e.g. Bitcoin).

---

**Step-by-Step Conceptual Explanation**  

1. Imagine a school register that is copied and stored by every class captain. If someone tries to change one copy, the others will show it is wrong. That is the basic idea of blockchain.  
2. Blockchain stores information in blocks that are linked together using cryptography.  
3. Because many computers hold the same record, it is very hard for one person to cheat the system.  
4. Although blockchain became famous through Bitcoin, it can also be used for land records, supply chain tracking, certificates and voting systems.  
5. Understanding blockchain prepares students for future digital opportunities in Nigeria and beyond.

---

**Practical / Worked Example**  
Possible uses in Nigeria:  
- Transparent land registration  
- Verifying WAEC or university certificates  
- Tracking agricultural products from farm to market  
- Secure digital identity

---

**Class Activity / Guided Practice**  
In groups, students discuss two problems in Nigeria that blockchain could help solve and present their ideas to the class.

---

**End-of-Lesson Quick Test**

2. What does “immutable” mean in blockchain?  
3. Name one use of blockchain besides cryptocurrency.  
4. Why is blockchain described as decentralised?  
5. True or False: Blockchain is controlled by only one company.
