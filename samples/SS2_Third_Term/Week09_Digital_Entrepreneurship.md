**Class:** SS2  
**Term:** Third Term  
**Week:** 9  
**Topic:** Digital Entrepreneurship

---

**Behavioral Learning Objectives**  
By the end of this lesson, students should be able to:  
1. Define digital entrepreneurship.  
2. List examples of digital business opportunities available to young Nigerians.  
3. Explain the basic steps in starting a simple online business.  
4. Identify skills needed for digital entrepreneurship.

---

**Key Concepts / Definitions**  
- **Digital Entrepreneurship**: Creating and running a business that primarily uses digital technologies and the Internet.  
- **E-commerce**: Buying and selling goods or services online.  
- **Freelancing**: Offering skills and services to clients on a project basis, often remotely.  
- **Digital product**: A product that is delivered electronically (e.g. e-books, online courses, graphics, software).

---

**Step-by-Step Conceptual Explanation**  

1. Many young people in Nigeria now earn money through their phones and laptops.  
2. Examples include selling products on Instagram or WhatsApp, offering graphic design, writing, programming, online tutoring, and creating content.  
3. Starting small is important. A student can begin with a skill they already have and offer it online.  
4. Key skills include basic digital literacy, communication, problem-solving, and consistency.  
5. Digital entrepreneurship can provide income, independence and valuable experience while still in school.

---

**Practical / Worked Example**  
Simple digital business ideas for students:  
- Selling printed notes or past questions  
- Offering phone photography and editing  
- Creating and selling simple Canva designs  
- Online tutoring for junior classes  
- Running a small WhatsApp-based thrift or food business

---

**Class Activity / Guided Practice**  
Students work in pairs to invent one realistic digital business idea that a secondary school student could start with little or no capital. They present the idea, target customers, and first three steps.

---

**End-of-Lesson Quick Test**  
1. What is digital entrepreneurship?  
2. Give two examples of digital business opportunities.  
3. What is freelancing?  
4. Name two skills needed for digital entrepreneurship.  
5. True or False: You need a large amount of money to start any digital business.

---

**Assignment**  
Write a one-page business idea for a digital product or service you could offer. Include: the problem it solves, who will buy it, and the first three steps you would take.
