**Class:** SS3  
**Term:** First Term  
**Week:** 9  
**Topic:** Internet of Things (IoT) Applications

---

**Behavioral Learning Objectives**  
By the end of this lesson, students should be able to:  
1. Define the Internet of Things (IoT).  
2. Explain how IoT devices communicate.  
3. Give examples of IoT applications in homes, cities and agriculture.  
4. Discuss one benefit and one challenge of IoT.

---

**Key Concepts / Definitions**  
- **Internet of Things (IoT)**: A network of physical devices embedded with sensors, software and connectivity that enables them to collect and exchange data.  
- **Sensor**: A device that detects changes in the environment (temperature, motion, light, etc.).  
- **Smart device**: A device that can connect to the Internet and interact with other devices or systems.  
- **Smart home**: A home equipped with IoT devices that can be controlled remotely.

---

**Step-by-Step Conceptual Explanation**  

1. Ordinary objects become “smart” when they can sense their environment and communicate over the Internet.  
2. Examples include smart bulbs, smart locks, fitness trackers, and sensors used on farms.  
3. In smart cities, IoT helps manage traffic, waste, energy and public safety.  
4. In agriculture, IoT sensors can monitor soil moisture and automatically control irrigation.  
5. While IoT brings convenience and efficiency, it also raises concerns about privacy and security.

---

**Practical / Worked Example**  
Everyday IoT examples students may already know:  
- Smartphones and smartwatches  
- CCTV cameras that send alerts  
- POS machines that communicate with banks  
- Weather stations used by farmers  
- Smart electricity meters

---

**Class Activity / Guided Practice**  
Students brainstorm five IoT applications that could solve real problems in their local community or school and share their ideas.

---

**End-of-Lesson Quick Test**  
1. What does IoT stand for?  
2. What is a sensor?  
3. Give two examples of IoT devices.  
4. State one benefit of IoT.  
5. True or False: IoT devices cannot connect to the Internet.
